notes:
  - term: "Casting the blocks"
    text: "杯珓 — a pair of wooden or bamboo crescents thrown before a shrine. One convex and one flat is yang, a yes; both flat is yin, a no; and some throws are void. It is the standard way of putting a question to a Chinese god, and people still do it. The whole story turns on a god who returns a false answer on purpose."
  - term: "Bearing the name of being wrong"
    text: "我宁受不灵之名，以救生人之命 — I would rather be called ineffective than take a life. Yuan Mei puts moral judgement above miraculous accuracy, which is rare: the god is praised by Heaven not for being right but for weighing consequences. It is a magistrate's argument, not a priest's."
  - term: "The franchise theory"
    text: "There is a shrine of Lord Guan in every village in the empire, and only one Lord Guan. The answer given here is that Heaven appoints a locally-upright ghost to run each shrine, while the real Guan Yu stands at God's left hand and cannot come down. This resolves a genuine problem in Chinese popular religion and explains a great deal of inconsistent temple practice."
  - term: "What this does to the pantheon"
    text: "Note the pattern across the collection: in 酆都知县 Bao Zheng and Guan Yu behave like colleagues in one hierarchy; here Guan Yu turns out to be a job description filled by different ghosts. Yuan Mei keeps asking how divinity is staffed, and his answers are always administrative rather than mystical."

Ma Feng of Liyang was a provincial graduate without office, tutoring in the house of a family named Li in the west village.

A neighbour named Wang was a violent man who beat his wife. His wife was starving and had nothing, and she stole a chicken from the Li household and cooked it. The Li family found out and told her husband. He was drunk. In a rage he took a knife and dragged his wife over, and once he had the facts out of her he was going to kill her.

In her terror the wife accused Ma Feng of stealing the chicken.

Ma argued and could not prove himself.

"There is a shrine of Lord Guan in the village," he said. "Let us go and cast the blocks. If they fall yin, the woman stole it. If they fall yang, the man did."

They cast three times. All three came up yang.

Wang dropped the knife and let his wife go home. And Ma Feng, on account of a stolen chicken, was despised by the whole village and lost his tutoring post for years.

Some time after, a medium was holding a planchette, and the god who came was Lord Guan. Ma Feng, remembering, roundly abused the god for his inaccuracy. The planchette wrote in the ash:

*Ma Feng. You will one day hold authority over people. Do you not understand that some matters are urgent and others can wait? You stealing a chicken costs you a post. That woman stealing a chicken dies at once under a knife. I would rather bear the name of being wrong and save a living life. God Above noted that I understand the weight of things, and promoted me three grades. And you resent me?*

Ma said: "Lord Guan has been made an emperor. What grades are left to rise?"

The god wrote:

*Now, in all the nine provinces there is a shrine of Lord Guan. How should there be enough Lords Guan to go round and share the offerings? Every village shrine has been set up by order of God Above, and in each one some ghost of that place who was upright in life is appointed to do the work. The real Lord Guan stands at the left hand of God. How would he have time to come down into the world?*

Ma Feng was convinced.
