The fisherman wakes, finds it strange, waits by the lake, and the corpse surfaces of its own accord — 赫然屍出, 'there it was, the corpse, out of the water.' In the last years of the Ming, the grand secretary He Fengsheng, hard pressed by Zhang Xianzhong at Wuchang, threw himself into Dunzi Lake and died.

From summer to autumn a god entrusted a dream to a certain lakeside resident, saying: "I have been charged by the Lord on High to guard Chancellor He's corpse, and it is bitterly hard; you may haul it up and look — the one with the mole on his left hand, that is he."

The man woke and found it strange. He waited by the lake, and there — out of the water it came, the corpse, plain to see. So he laid it out and buried it.

The body had been in the water a hundred and seventy days, and its face was like that of a living man.
