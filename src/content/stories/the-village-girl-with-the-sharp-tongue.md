  村尽处，见竹篱半架，左有双黑扉，一女郎倚扉斜立，捉风中絮搓掌上，嗤嗤憨笑。陈睨之，魂飞色夺，因兜搭与语。女郎不怒亦不答，但呼阿母来。亡何，一驼背媪出，问女何为。女曰：“不知何处来一莽汉，烦絮煞人。”陈意窘，诡以乞浆告。媪曰：“斗碗难容客坐。小慧，取一盏凉水来！”女嗷声而进。陈曰：“令爱年几何矣？”媪曰：“但记其生年属虎，不知今当几何岁也！”问：“婿家为谁？”媪曰：“老身残废，止此一女，留伴膝下，不欲遣事他人。”陈曰：“女生有家，膝下非长计也。”适女取凉水至，闻余语，大声谓媪曰：“是客不怀好意，毋多谈！”媪笑曰：“可听则听，是诚在我，婢子何必琐琐。”陈乃夸状元以歆动之。媪俯思良久，曰：“状元是何物？”陈曰：“读书成进士，名魁金榜，入词垣，掌制诰，以文章华国，为天下第一人，是名状元。”媪曰：“不知第一人，几年一出？”曰：“三年。”女从旁微晒曰：“吾谓状元，是千古第一人，原来只三年一个！此等脚色，也向人喋喋不休，大是怪事！”媪叱曰：“小妖婢嚣薄嘴，动辄翘人短处。”女曰：“干侬甚事，痴儿自取病耳！”一笑竟去。
  陈惘然久之，继而谓媪曰：“如不弃嫌，敬留薄聘。”脱囊中双南金予之。媪手摩再四，曰：“嗅之不馨，握之辄冰，是何物哉？”陈曰：“此名黄金。汝辈得之，寒可作衣，饥可作食，真世宝也！”媪曰：“吾家有桑百株，有田半顷，颇不忧冻馁，是物恐此间无用处，还留状元郎作用度。”掷之地曰：“可惜风魔儿，全无一点大雅相，徒以财势恐吓人耳！”言毕，阖扉而进，陈痴立半晌，嗟叹而返。

My uncle-by-marriage, Chen Yongzhai, had topped the empire in the jichou year and was on leave returning south. He reached Tianshui Post; beside the road was a small village, green trees in thick shade, wild crabapple in bloom — and he delighted in it, and walked on alone, forgetting the distance.

At the village's end he saw a bamboo fence half-framed, with a double black gate to the left, and a girl leaning by the gate, twisting wind-blown catkins in her palm, giggling foolishly. Chen eyed her, soul flown; he struck up talk. The girl neither angered nor answered, only called: "Mother, come." Presently a hunchbacked old woman came and asked what the girl was doing. The girl said: "Don't know where a boor came from — so tiresome!" Chen, abashed, falsely said he had come for water. The woman said: "A bowl can't seat a guest. Xiaohui, bring a cup of cool water!" The girl cried and went in.

Chen said: "How old is your daughter?" The woman said: "I only know she was born in the year of the tiger; who knows her age now!" He asked: "Who is the son-in-law?" She said: "I am crippled; only this one daughter; I keep her by my knee and do not wish to send her to serve another." Chen said: "A girl has a home; the knee is not a long plan." As the girl brought the water she heard and cried to the woman: "This guest means no good; do not talk more!" The woman laughed: "Listen if you will; it is indeed up to me; why should the maiden be so fussy?"

Chen then boasted of being top graduate to move her. The woman pondered: "What is a top graduate?" Chen said: "Study makes you a 进士, your name tops the golden list, you enter the Hanlin, hold the edicts, adorn the realm with writing — first man under heaven; that is a top graduate." The woman said: "Don't know — the first man; how often does one appear?" Chen said: "Every three years."

The girl, from the side, smiled: "I thought a top graduate was the first man through a thousand ages; turns out only one every three years! This kind of fellow, also babbling to people — a great oddity!" The woman chided: "Little demon-girl, frivolous mouth, always picks at people's faults." The girl said: "What's it to me? The fool brings it on himself!" She laughed and left.

Chen, at a loss, then said to the woman: "If you do not disdain, I leave a modest bride-price." He took two bars of fine gold from his pouch. The woman rubbed it: "Smells not fragrant, holds icy — what is it?" Chen said: "This is called gold. You get it, cold can be clothes, hunger can be food, truly a world treasure!" The woman said: "My family has a hundred mulberries and half an acre of field; not worried about cold or hunger. This thing has no use here; keep it for the top graduate to spend." She threw it to the ground: "Pity the madman — not a trace of elegance, only bullying with wealth and power!" Finished, she closed the gate and went in. Chen stood dumb, sighed, and returned.

*The Duo comment: Gold in a yellow mouth, a black-gauze cap of power. How many men, ancient and modern, have had their proud bones worn away by this — yet in the inner chambers there is such a humorous person! Beneath the pomegranate skirt, one should kowtow three thousand times.*
