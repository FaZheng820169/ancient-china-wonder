Jiang Liangfu of Nancheng trained in the Confucian way but did not succeed, growing old in a village school. In the tenth year of the Chunxi era he fell ill and died, then revived after half a day.

He told his family: "I was just now summoned to the court of the dead. I stood below the dais in the courtyard, and by chance the King of Hell had not yet come out to hold session, so I was granted a temporary return — but it cannot last long either."

After two double-hours he finally died for good.
