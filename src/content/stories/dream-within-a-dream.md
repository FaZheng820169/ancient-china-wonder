  曾孝廉赴南宫试，挈一老仆，束转北上，夜投留智庙。时已昏暮，解鞍即憩。偶步门外，见垂杨夹岸，长板红桥，斜横春水。旁杏花数十树，有翠鸟啁啾其上。曾踏桥度岸，见一家园门洞开。徐步而入，文宙窈窕，绣阁参差。循廊曲折，直达内寝。珠箔数重，琼钩斜卷。水晶屏后，设珊瑚床一具，海红帐垂垂未下。角枕锦衾，麝兰喷溢。左横梳妆小几，镜匣未收，粉奁半启。胆瓶内碧桃小瓣，妥落脂合旁。闻翘凤声琐碎而至，曾惊匿夹幕间。视之，闺中细君也。曾问：“何得来此？”笑曰：“此郎君新购之别墅，何善忘耶？”曾亦不复省忆。联坐狎谈，忽外厢马腾人沸。起询之，盖迎新殿撰赶杏园宴者。曾即跨鞍，驺从导去。十里花尘，万家钿阁，金鞭玉勒，顾盼自豪。宴罢而归，夫人迎门相候。焚香燃烛，话昔年寒窗夜读，相对各有喜色。
  已而就寝，私念夫人年齿稍长，今富贵若此，何不广列金钗以充下陈？方伏枕，即有一人投刺门下，云是富家某交结新贵，特以十斛珠购美姬四名，备充妾媵。曾大喜，立命召入。亡何，曾自黛绿，侍立满前，燕瘦环肥，并皆佳妙，曾恐夫人娇妒，引入别院，询其小字，丰肌者曰娟娟，文弱者曰楚楚，明眸秀靥者曰倩桃，垂发掠作斜鬓者曰春柳。某命娟娟展褥，楚楚抱衾，倩桃列绣枕，春柳代除冠服。某先裸体入帏，回视诸姬，纷纷卸装，解罗襦，缓绣裙，脱鸦头袜、合欢鞋子，解绛结，提桃花裈，雪肉粉肌，争来就宿。须臾，左香右黛，玉体横陈，八瓣香莲，高抬竞举。某心摇摇，不知所向。
  忽闻脚后夫人高唤，春梦顿醒，因大嗔曰：“尔何太絮聒？方便片时，温柔乡早入梦也。”夫人亦诮让。曾愤甚，曰：“我当日寒贱时，跬步辄加约束。今幸大富贵，汉家自有制度，岂由燕支虎风流棒喝哉？”夫人着衣起，向壁而哭曰：“薄幸儿记否？汝失馆时，至晚不得一顿粥，拔侬压鬓钗质钱易斗目，今骤得志，动辄加白眼，结发情何在也？”曾正以新贵自居，闻夫人摘旧日瑕，尤负气不肯下，拍枕大呼曰：“一副五花诰，看汝何颜消受也？”
  倏闻耳畔有笑声曰：“相公梦魇耶！”纽枕回视，一老仆向灯下捉襟捕虱而已。一凝神半晌，拥被大笑，仆竟茫然。
  铎曰：“人当春梦醒，未有不失笑者，岂知身犹在梦中耶？惟至人无梦，因其无富贵心，亦惟愚人无梦，因其无富贵福。”

A successful candidate named Zeng was on his way to the metropolitan exam; he took an old servant and travelled north, and at night lodged at Liuzhi Temple. Dusk had fallen; he unsaddled and rested. Strolling outside, he saw willows along a bank, a long red bridge over spring water, and dozens of apricot trees with kingfishers chirping among them. Zeng crossed the bridge and came to a garden gate standing open. He wandered in, through elegant chambers, winding by the corridor to the inner bedroom — pearl blinds, coral bed, sea-red curtains not yet drawn. On a small dressing-table the mirror-case lay open, the powder-box half-unclosed, a peach petal by the rouge. He heard the patter of phoenix slippers; startled, he hid in the side curtain. It was his own wife.

He asked: "How came you here?"

She smiled: "This is the villa you newly bought; how forgetful!"

He did not recall. They sat and talked; then outside, horses neighed and men buzzed. He asked — it was the welcome of the new top graduate to the Apricot Garden feast. Zeng mounted and was escorted away: ten li of flower-dust, a proud glance. After the feast he returned; his wife waited at the door. They burned incense, spoke of old cold-window nights; both looked pleased.

Then to bed. He thought his wife was older now, and they so rich — why not add concubines to the lower ranks? As he laid his head, a man came with a card: a rich family seeking to connect with the new dignitary, offering ten measures of pearls to buy four beauties as concubines. Zeng was delighted, and summoned them. They came — plump Juanjuan, delicate Chuchu, bright-eyed Qiantao, the hair-tossing Chunliu. He had Juanjuan spread the mattress, Chuchu hold the quilt, Qiantao arrange the pillows, Chunliu remove his crown. He entered the curtain naked; the beauties undressed around him — a scene of jade flesh. He was dizzy with it.

Suddenly he heard his wife calling from behind the bed; the spring dream broke. He was angry: "Why so nagging? A moment's convenience, and the land of warmth was already a dream." The wife also chided. He, furious, said: "When I was poor, every step was constrained. Now fortunate and great, the Han house has its own system — must it be ruled by the rose-thorn rod of your jealousy?" The wife rose and wept to the wall: "Faithless one, remember? When you lost your post, you couldn't get a bowl of congee at night; you pawned my hairpin for cash to buy a measure of rice. Now suddenly successful, you cast white eyes — where is the marriage-bond?" He, holding the new-dignitary airs, hearing her bring up old flaws, grew more defiant, slapped the pillow and cried: "A five-colour mandate — see if you have the face to enjoy it!"

Suddenly a laugh by his ear: "Sir, are you having a nightmare!"

He turned from the pillow; it was the old servant catching lice by the lamp. He composed himself; holding the quilt, he laughed. The servant was blank.

*The Duo comment: When a man wakes from the spring dream, none but laughs — who knows he is still in a dream? Only the perfect man has no dream, because he has no wealth-heart; and only the fool has no dream, because he has no wealth-fortune.*
