Death by lightning in this cosmology is an execution with a date, carried out by the Thunder Bureau, not an accident. Compare 《胡煌僕》, where the same office is persuaded to postpone a bolt by seventy days."

This is a story about the limits of a small god's discretion, and it explains why the solution has to be procedural rather than miraculous."

    text: "凡太夫人平日一饮一馔、一帐一衣，务使十倍其数，浪费而暴餮之，庶几禄尽则亡 — whatever she drinks and eats, whatever bed-hangings and clothes she uses, let it be ten times as much, let her waste and gorge, so that her allotment of 禄 is spent out and she dies. 禄 is the fixed credit of food, clothing and comfort assigned to a life; running it out ends the life. The remedy is therefore a kind of accounting: bring the death forward on one line so that it escapes the other. In the reign of Kangxi, Wang Yixin, magistrate of Shitai, was on the closest terms with a friend named Lin. Lin died, and became the earth god of Shitai. Every night, though the living and the dead are apart, the two of them kept company as they had in life.

The earth god said to Wang privately: "Your family has a calamity coming, and I dare not keep it from you; but once I have told you, I am afraid I shall not escape Heaven's punishment."

Wang pressed him again and again. At last he said: "Your honoured mother is destined to be struck by lightning."

Wang was horrified, and wept and begged for a way out. The god said: "This is a debt of evil from a former life. My office is low and my rank small — how could I save her?"

Wang wept and pleaded without stopping. The god said: "There is only one way. Set about the duties of a filial son at once. Whatever your mother eats and drinks, whatever bed-hangings and clothes she uses — make it ten times as much. Let her waste and gorge, so that her allotted fortune runs out and she dies; then she may end well, and though the thunder comes it will be of no use."

Wang did as he was told, and his mother died within a few years.

Three years after that the sky rained, and the thunder came indeed. It circled the coffin, lighting it up, and the whole room smelled of sulphur. But it did not strike. It broke out through the roof, flew across and struck the earth god's shrine. The clay image was reduced to mud.
