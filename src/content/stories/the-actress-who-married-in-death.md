  磬儿，珠市梁四家女伶也。粱四妇本吴倡，善琵琶，及归梁，买雏姬教梨园为活。磬儿意不屑，辄逃塾。假母日棰楚，诸姊妹竞劝之。磬儿曰：“若从我，须以旦脚改净色。”问其故。曰：“我不幸为女儿身，有恨无所吐。若作净色，犹可借英雄面目，一泄胸中块垒耳！”由是《千金记》诸杂剧，磬儿独冠场。
  孝廉詹湘亭待诏白门，偕友寓梁四家，夜演《千金记》至《别姬》诸剧，女皆意属虞姬。而湘亭独以楚重瞳为妩媚，群起哗笑之。及卸装，视老霸王姿容，果高出帐下美人上，遂叹服。
  明日，张筳海棠树下，青衫红粉，团围错坐。磬儿本歙产，湘亭亦婺源籍，两人各操土音，以道其倾慕。而座上诸友，相对微笑，竞不解刺刺作何语。已而湘亭志眉中目，不能得中翰，诸友尽返桌，而湘亭束装未发，意不忘磬儿也。思欲买桃叶桨，载与俱归。而梁家方居为奇货，且欲留压班头；有非百万缠头，不能摇夺者。相对泫然，焦思无计。磐儿忽私语曰：“君何计之拙也？彼所以居奇不售者，以我为钱树子耳！君去，妾必不生。留骏骨而买之，定不须千金值矣！”湘亭大悲。不得已，珍重而别。
  归未两月，闻磬儿病且死。湘亭曰：“花前一诺，信同抱柱矣！卿不负我，我岂敢负卿哉？”急赴金陵，以三百金买柩而回，葬于桐泾桥北。王夫人曹墨琴志其墓；请名士挽以诗词；予谱《干金笑》传奇付诸乐部，噫！不能生事，而以死归，殆钟情者不得已之极思乎？而磬儿亦自此不死矣！

Qinger was a girl actor of the Liang family troupe in the Pearl Market. Liang's wife had been a Wu singsong-girl, skilled at the pipa; after she married Liang she bought young actresses and taught them opera to live. Qinger disdained it and often ran from lessons. The foster-mother beat her daily; the sisters urged her. Qenger said: "If you follow me, you must change from the female lead to the painted-face." Asked why, she said: "I was born a girl, with grievances I cannot vent. If I play the painted-face, I can borrow a hero's face to pour out the blockage in my chest." And so in the 千金记 cycle Qenger alone topped the cast.

The successful candidate Zhan Xiangting, awaiting appointment at Baimen, lodged with friends at Liang's. At night they performed the 千金记 through to the Farewell-My-Concubine scene; the girls all favoured Yu Ji, but Zhan alone found the Chu double-pupil — Xiang Yu — charming, and all laughed at him. When she removed her makeup, the old King Xiang's looks indeed surpassed the beauties under the canopy, and Zhan sighed in admiration.

Next day a feast was spread beneath the crabapple; blue-robed and red-powdered sat intermingled. Qinger was from She, Zhan from Wuyuan; the two spoke their own dialects, telling of their admiration, while the friends only smiled, not understanding a word. Then Zhan, with brow and eye, could not win a mid-rank post, and the friends left the table; but Zhan had not packed, his heart still with Qenger. He thought to buy a peach-leaf boat and take her home. But the Liang family held her as a rare commodity and wanted to keep her as lead — nothing short of a fortune in tips could move them. They faced each other in tears, no plan.

Qenger whispered: "Why so clumsy? The reason they hold her dear is that I am their cash-cow! If you leave, I surely will not live. Keep the fine bones and buy her — it will not cost a thousand." Zhan was grieved; reluctantly, they parted with ceremony.

Less than two months later, word came that Qenger was ill and dying. Zhan said: "The promise before the flowers — as faithful as holding the pillar! You did not fail me; how dare I fail you?" He rushed to Jinling, bought her coffin for three hundred taels, and brought it home, burying her north of Tongjing Bridge. Lady Wang Cao Moqin wrote her epitaph; famous men sent elegies; I myself wrote the 千金笑 legend for the troupe. Alas — could not live together, but returned in death. Is this not the extremity of a passionate heart? And Qenger, from this, never died.

*The Duo comment: A man with a seven-foot frame, dull and without noble conduct, ends rotting with the grass — why in the inner chambers is there one who transmits her name? Only by not failing the death-promise and living can one embrace life and die. At the same time there was a He'er who gave a portrait of Ma Xianglan to a Zhao of Wujiang — also a wise-hearted girl — and later followed a wastrel, widowed in half a year, still leaning on a foster-mother to sell the pipa. Alas! The peach has fallen on Xue Tao's grave, the swallow returned empty to Guan Pan's tower. He'er's life is not as good as Qenger's death.*
