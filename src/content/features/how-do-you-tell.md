A man meets a woman in the hills. She is beautiful, she is naked, and he has spent his life sleeping out on the mountains. He knows at once that she is not human and shows no fear whatsoever.

What he does next is the interesting part. He shouts at her:

> 汝鬼邪怪邪
>
> Are you a **ghost**, or are you a **sprite**?

That is from 《王二》. He does not ask if she is real. He asks which column she goes in, because the answer determines what can be done about her — a 鬼 is a dead person and a 怪 is a thing that never was one, and the two require entirely different handling. She answers with another smile and says nothing.

The genre is full of this. Nobody in these stories is ever in doubt for long that something is going on; the question is always *what sort*, and there is a working kit of tests for finding out. It is not a mystical kit. It is closer to a field guide.

## 1. Ask it to its face

The direct question comes first, and the answer is frequently silence. In 《木箍頸》 a hunter asks a three-foot man in the wilds 何人 — who are you? — and gets 搖頭不語, a shake of the head and nothing said. Yuan Mei's footnote explains why it matters: 能呼其名，則不為害 — if you can call a thing by its name, it has no power to harm you.

The refusal to be classified is not evasion. It is the finding. A creature that will not give its category cannot be filed, summoned, or driven off — and the stories treat that as a diagnosis.

## 2. Look for smoke

Wang Er goes back to the woman's cave and finds it deep, still, elegant — and 不聞烟火氣, with no smell of cooking fire about it. Twenty years later, living in his house, 終不火食: she never once ate cooked food.

The hearth is the boundary. A human household cooks; a non-human one does not, and the test is olfactory. Where Western detection runs on holy objects, this one runs on whether dinner is being made.

## 3. Look at the feet

Going up to the cave, 女不穿屨，步武如飛 — she wore no shoes and went as though flying, while Wang's legs gave out on the stony path. Bare feet and inhuman speed, noted by a professional hunter who is simply watching how she moves.

## 4. Can anybody else see her?

This is the test that gets run most often, and it is run by committee. In 《五郎君》 a wife is plainly talking to someone every night, so 家衆穴隙潛窺，無所視 — the household bores holes through the cracks and peeps, and sees nothing at all.

In 《衢州少婦》 the test goes the other way: the whole crowd seizes her in the hall, and she 化為青狐奔而出 — becomes a blue fox and runs for the gate. Visibility is not a property of the creature. It is a property of the situation, and you find out which by fetching other people.

## 5. Watch the thresholds

Door gods are not decoration. In 《張鮎魚》 an underworld runner is sent back to a house for money and reports on the way in: 初時左門神見拒，而右戶尉為我通報，故得之 — at first the door-god on the left refused me, but the door-warden on the right announced me, and so I got it.

Two gods on one gate, and they did not give the same answer. A threshold is a checkpoint, and checkpoints are staffed by people who vary.

## 6. Set a physical trap

This is the most satisfying test in the collection, and it is pure craft rather than ritual. In 《茶僕崔三》 a hunter comes to visit his brother, notices that the woman visiting every night stops coming the moment he arrives, asks which way she enters, and then 取獵具，捲網數枚，散布之 — fetches his hunting gear, spreads several rolled nets, and waits at the stair.

After the third watch there is a creak. He lights a torch. There is a spotted wildcat, three feet long, dead in the net.

## 7. Smell it

The same woman announces herself by scent: 覺異香馥烈 — he became aware of a strange fragrance, rich and strong, and there she was by the lamp. Supernatural presence in these stories has a signature smell, and the nose is a legitimate instrument.

## 8. Check the clock

《五郎君》's visitor comes 每至更深, whenever the night grows deep. 《衢州少婦》 meets her man in the hour before the drum tower's third watch. Wang Er's woman goes off into the hills 經月不返 and returns on no schedule anyone can predict.

The pattern matters more than the apparition. In 《衢州少婦》 it is not a priest who works it out but the household: 李生家訝李連日宵行 — the family wondered that he had been going out every single night, and concluded there must be a woman. Detection in this genre is usually social before it is spectral.

## 9. Wait, and see if it ages

In 《蜀中道人》 a Taoist reads a young official's future in his voice and his walk, and then, thirty years later, the official meets him again — unchanged. Immortals fail the test that everything alive passes. Time is the assay, and it is slow, which is why this test is used least and trusted most.

## 10. Follow the money

The most reliable tell in the whole library. 《茶僕崔三》: the woman produces 官劵十千 — government notes for ten thousand — for a servant earning a thousand cash a month. 《呂使君宅》: a parting gift of 白金百兩 and a horse. 《五郎君》: 金帛錢綺盈室 — gold and silk, cash and damask filling the room.

Nobody in these stories asks where the money came from and then stops asking. Wealth with no visible source is the tell that the household itself notices first, and it is the one most often deliberately ignored — see the husband in 《五郎君》, who is furious, and then does the arithmetic, and does not ask again.

## 11. If all else fails, summon it

When observation runs out, you hire procedure. In 《三趙失舟》 an exorcist performs 考召, the summons-and-interrogation: the rite is barely finished when the house is full of spirits, and the one at their head turns out to be the helpful farmer who gave directions that morning. He is the local earth god, he is carrying a warrant from the City God, and the exorcist reads the warrant and can find no charge to bring.

That is the real answer to "how do you tell": you make it appear and you ask it, under procedure, in front of witnesses. Everything before that is field observation.

## What is not in the kit

For anyone coming to this material from Western supernatural fiction, the absences are the surprise. There is no holy water. There is no mirror test — mirrors barely appear. There is no iron that burns, no sacred word, no amulet that works by being worn. The iron in 《木箍頸》 is a neck brace, not a weapon. The objects in the [instrument cabinet](/features/the-instrument-cabinet) are tools for *doing*, not for detecting.

What the kit contains instead is observation, company, thresholds, traps, clocks, ledgers and — when it comes to it — a summons. It is an empirical kit, and it belongs to a culture that assumed the strange was real and therefore testable.

## Why the test fails, and why that is better

The sharpest thing about these tests is how often they do not settle anything.

In 《茶僕崔三》 the hunter's net works perfectly. It catches a wildcat three feet long. His brother skins it and cooks it. And that night the woman is standing by the lamp again, furious, and her account of the loss is: 幸我是時未離家，僅殺了一婢，壞衫子一領而已 — luckily I had not left the house just then; all you managed was one of my maids killed, and a single gown spoiled.

They killed the wrong body. The test produced a corpse and the corpse was staff.

This happens because the kit was never designed to *resolve*. It was designed to classify, and classification is the first step of a procedure, not the end of a story. See [She Is There Still](/worldview/she-is-there-still) for what happens when the classification is complete and nothing closes anyway — 至今猶存, *she is there still*, is the ending these tales reach for far more often than any exposure.

## For writers and game masters

Three things follow.

**Give your reader a test, and make it mundane.** The best detection scenes here involve smoke, shoes, a clock and a smell. A character who checks whether the house cooks is doing something your reader can picture, and the domesticity makes the wrongness land harder than any ritual.

**Let the test be run by other people.** The household peeping through the cracks, the family noticing the nightly exits, the brother asking which door she uses — detection here is a group activity, and group detection gives you scenes instead of revelations.

**Let it fail, and let the failure be specific.** Not "the test didn't work" but "you caught something, and it was the maid." A test that returns a true result and the wrong answer is worth three that work.
